# A Methodological Framework for Sourcing High-Quality Public Domain Texts for Digital Applications  
**Case Studies: Confucius, Lao Tzu, Mahatma Gandhi, Nikola Tesla, Archimedes**

***

## I. Introduction: Curating the Public Domain for Modern Audiences

### Objective and Rationale

The central objective of this report is to establish a **verified and high-quality corpus of public domain texts** for the Vimarsh digital application, focusing on the works of five seminal figures: Confucius, Lao Tzu, Mahatma Gandhi, Nikola Tesla, and Archimedes. The public domain represents a vast cultural commons, a repository of human knowledge and creativity **free from copyright restrictions**. For modern digital platforms like Vimarsh, this commons is not merely a source of free content but a dynamic resource that, when carefully curated, can provide audiences with direct access to *foundational texts* of global philosophy, science, and history.  
This report provides a **methodological framework** for such curation, addressing the unique archival challenges presented by each figure and offering a clear, actionable pathway for the legal and ethical acquisition of their works.

### Methodological Challenges

- **Translation Dilemma**: For figures like Confucius, Lao Tzu, and Archimedes, the copyright status of the translation is as critical as that of the original text.
- **Navigating the Archival Labyrinth**: Prolific modern figures such as Gandhi and Tesla have extensive materials scattered across institutional and governmental archives.
- **Copyright Complexity**: Legal definitions, term lengths, and national jurisdictions create nuanced landscapes; notable cases like Martin Luther King, Jr. illustrate the need for *rigorous verification*.

### Structure of the Report

Sections are dedicated to each figure, grouped thematically by their distinct **archival persona**:
- *Translated Sage*: Confucius and Lao Tzu
- *National Icon*: Mahatma Gandhi
- *Prolific Inventor*: Nikola Tesla
- *Reconstructed Genius*: Archimedes

This framework moves beyond mere lists of sources to analyze *how historical knowledge is accessed and curated in the digital age*.

## II. Sourcing the Foundational Texts of Eastern Philosophy: Confucius and Lao Tzu

### 2.1. The Analects and Classics of Confucius

#### Identifying the Core Corpus

- **The Analects** (Lúnyǔ): Core compilation.
- **The Four Books**: Recommended for completeness *(includes The Great Learning, Doctrine of the Mean, Works of Mencius)*.

#### The Seminal Translator: James Legge

- **James Legge's** multi-volume "The Chinese Classics" (1860s) is the authoritative public domain translation.
- Extensive introductions, explanatory notes, and scholarly apparatus.

#### Primary Digital Repositories

## III. Navigating the Archives of a Modern Leader: The Works of Mahatma Gandhi

### 3.1. The Definitive Collection: The CWMG

- **The Collected Works of Mahatma Gandhi (CWMG)**:  
  - 100 volumes, 50,000+ pages, 1884–1948.
  - State-sponsored, definitive resource.

#### Digital Access

- **Gandhi Heritage Portal**  
  - [Complete CWMG](https://www.gandhiheritageportal.org/the-collected-works-of-mahatma-gandhi)
- **GandhiServe Foundation**  
  - [Download PDFs](https://www.gandhiserve.net/about-mahatma-gandhi/collected-works-of-mahatma-gandhi/)
- **Internet Archive**  
  - [Vol. 1](https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.100764)
  - [Vol. 35](https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.32803)
- **Wikisource**  
  - [100 Volumes Transcription](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Collected_Works_of_Mahatma_Gandhi)

#### Copyright Status

- Legally public domain in the USA (many volumes published pre-1930; Gandhi died 1948).
- Digitizing institutions apply *Creative Commons* licenses (CC BY-NC-ND).
- Commercial use in the USA is permitted (ethical attribution recommended).

### 3.2. Key Individual Works

#### Autobiography: The Story of My Experiments with Truth

- **Publication History**: Originally serialized 1925-1929, then published in book form in 1927/1929 (public domain in USA).
- **Recommended Editions**:
  - [Standard Ebooks (1940 revised)](https://standardebooks.org/ebooks/mahatma-gandhi/the-story-of-my-experiments-with-truth/mahadev-desai)
  - [mkgandhi.org PDF](https://www.mkgandhi.org/ebks/An-Autobiography.pdf)

## VI. Conclusion and Recommendations for Implementation

### Synthesis of Findings

- Each historical figure requires a **bespoke archival strategy** ("archival persona").
- Use **definitive editions**: e.g., CWMG, Legge translations, Heath's Archimedes.
- Implement **cross-verification** between ebook sources and high-fidelity scans.
- Always **attribute** sources, translators, and curators.
- Structure metadata: repository, edition, translator, volume, publication date.

### Best Practices for Vimarsh App

1. **Prioritize Definitive Editions**
2. **Cross-verify Sources**
3. **Provide Attribution**
4. **Use Structured Metadata**
